DC Metro Intergroup

An intergroup is a service body made up of representatives from local OA meetings. We exist to help groups carry the OA message to compulsive eaters who still suffer.

Business meetings

The DC Metro Intergroup business meeting is held on the second Monday of every odd month, 5:30–6:30 PM — January, March, May, July, September, and November. All OA members are welcome to attend.

Each group is encouraged to send a representative (Intergroup Rep, or IR) to vote on intergroup business. Visiting OA members can attend as observers any time.

What we do

The intergroup supports its member meetings by:

  • Maintaining and publishing the DC metro meeting list
  • Organizing local workshops, retreats, and newcomer outreach
  • Producing Together We Can, our local newsletter
  • Seeding funding for new meetings (rent, literature)
  • Doing public information and professional outreach
  • Sending representatives to OA Region and World Service

Service positions

The intergroup is run entirely by OA member volunteers. Open service positions are announced at each business meeting; service is one of the Tools of Recovery and a powerful way to strengthen your own program.

Curious about a position? Contact us — we'd love to hear from you.

Self-support

OA is fully self-supporting and accepts contributions only from OA members. The intergroup needs help to keep our rooms open — virtual and in-person — provide opportunities for fellowship, and carry the message to compulsive eaters who still suffer.
