DC Metro Intergroup

An intergroup is the service body that supports local OA meetings. We help meetings carry the message, support newcomers, host events, and represent our area at OA Region and World Service.

What is intergroup?

DC Metro Intergroup is a volunteer-run service body made up of representatives from local OA meetings across Washington DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia. We meet regularly, vote on shared business, and pool resources to help every meeting thrive.

Each meeting is encouraged to send an Intergroup Representative (IR) who carries the group's voice and reports back. Any OA member is welcome to attend as an observer.

Intergroup goals

What we do for you and the meetings you attend:

  • Maintain and publish the DC metro meeting list
  • Host workshops, retreats, and newcomer outreach
  • Produce Together We Can, our local newsletter
  • Seed funding for new meetings — rent and starter literature
  • Run public information and outreach to healthcare professionals
  • Send representatives to OA Region and World Service Business Conference
  • Operate the phone line, email, and this website

Why participate? Why we serve.

Service is one of the Tools of Recovery — and for many of us, it's where our own recovery deepened. The Twelfth Step ends with carrying the message; participating in intergroup is one way to live that out.

You don't need years of recovery to serve. There's a role for every member, from passing out literature at a meeting to chairing a committee. It works when we do.

Service opportunities

We almost always have open positions. A few examples:

  • Intergroup Representative (IR) — bring your group's voice to business meetings.
  • Newcomer outreach — answer the phone line, greet at meetings, run newcomer events.
  • Meeting list maintainer — keep the Where & When current.
  • Public information — reach professionals and the public.
  • Treasurer support — track contributions and pay expenses.
  • Workshop and retreat planning — organize events.
  • Webmaster — maintain this website.

Interested? Come to a business meeting or email any of the role-based addresses below.

Sign up for intergroup emails

Two ways to stay informed:

  • Meeting reminders — send a blank email to OA-DCMetro-Meeting-Reminders+subscribe@DCmetro-OAIG.groups.io.
  • Intergroup list-serv — join our groups.io list for general announcements.

Next intergroup business meeting

DC Metro Intergroup meets on the second Monday of every odd month, 5:30–6:30 PM — January, March, May, July, September, and November.

All OA members are welcome. Email whereandwhen@oa-dcmetro.org for the Zoom link.
