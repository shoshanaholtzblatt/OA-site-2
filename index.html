What is Overeaters Anonymous?

OA is a community of people who suffer from the disease of compulsive overeating. Our fellowship offers physical, emotional, and spiritual recovery for those who want to stop eating compulsively. We use the Twelve Steps adapted from Alcoholics Anonymous.

OA is not a diet club. We don't endorse any particular food plan. Whatever your food issue — compulsive overeating, undereating, food addiction, anorexia, bulimia, binge eating, or overexercising — you're welcome here.

There are no dues or fees for members; we are self-supporting through our own contributions. OA is not affiliated with any public or private organization, political movement, ideology, or religious doctrine.
