Overeaters Anonymous · Washington DC Metro

You are not alone in this.

Overeaters Anonymous is a fellowship of people who, through shared experience, strength, and hope, are recovering from compulsive eating and food behaviors. There are no dues, no fees, and everyone is welcome — whatever your relationship with food.

Meetings

What is a meeting?

Meetings are gatherings of two or more compulsive eaters who come together to share their experience, strength, and hope. Most last 60–90 minutes. You don't need to share, identify yourself, or commit to anything — you can just listen.

For newcomers

Do I qualify?

If your relationship with food feels out of your control — bingeing, restricting, obsessing, hiding it from others — you may have come to the right place. OA welcomes anyone with a desire to stop eating compulsively.

If you're not sure, our 15-question self-test on the Newcomers page can help you think it through.

Behind the scenes

What is Intergroup? Why serve?

DC Metro Intergroup is the service body that supports OA meetings across DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia. We maintain the meeting list, host workshops and retreats, do outreach to newcomers and professionals, and represent our area at OA Region and World Service.

Service is one of the Tools of Recovery — and for many of us, it's where recovery deepened. Whether it's putting away chairs, answering the phone line, sponsoring a newcomer, or sitting on the board, there's a place for you.
