Welcome — we're glad you're here

If your eating is out of control, you're not alone. Many of us came to our first meeting feeling exactly the way you might feel now. There is a way out, and it doesn't cost anything to start.

What to expect at your first meeting

OA meetings are gatherings of two or more compulsive eaters who come together to share their experience, strength, and hope. Most meetings last 60 to 90 minutes and follow a simple format: readings from OA literature, members sharing what's working for them, and time at the end for questions.

You don't need to share, identify yourself, or commit to anything. You can just listen. There is no weigh-in, no diet plan, and no fee.

The Twelve Steps

OA's recovery program is built on the Twelve Steps adapted from Alcoholics Anonymous. You'll hear members reference them at every meeting. You don't need to know them, agree with them, or work them to be welcome — but you'll find they're at the heart of how we recover.

Read the Twelve Steps →

A note on anonymity

Anonymity is the spiritual foundation of our program. We never share who attends meetings, what they say, or any personal information outside the room. You can use just your first name, and you can trust that what you share is held in confidence.
