Seventh Tradition

"Every OA group ought to be fully self-supporting, declining outside contributions."

Why we contribute

OA accepts contributions only from OA members. Your contribution keeps our rooms open — virtual and in-person — funds local workshops and retreats, and helps us carry the message to compulsive eaters who still suffer.

Many of us give what we couldn't afford to spend on food in our compulsive years. Whatever you give, your support matters.

Where your contribution goes

  • Meeting space rent for in-person groups
  • Literature and newcomer pamphlets
  • Local workshops, retreats, and the annual newsletter
  • Public information and outreach to professionals
  • Representation at OA Region and World Service
  • Website, phone line, and email infrastructure

The traditions of self-support

The Seventh Tradition is one of OA's twelve traditions — principles that keep our fellowship unified, independent, and focused on its primary purpose. By supporting OA ourselves, we keep the program free of outside influence and free of charge for the still-suffering compulsive eater.
